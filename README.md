<h2>Features 🛠️</h2>
<h3> Browsing and shopping </h3>
<ul>
 
<li>Home page listing every product </li>
<li>Filter by category and by brand</li>
<li>Search by product name or brand</li>
<li>Product pages</li>
<li>Stock display on product pages, including a message when stock runs low (10 or fewer)</li>
<li>Currency switcher between euro and two other currencies, with prices converted automatically</li>
<li>Light and dark mode toggle</li>
</ul>
<h3>Accounts</h3>
<ul>
<li>Register and log in, with passwords stored hashed </li>
<li>Log out, which clears the whole session</li>
<li>Account page to save your name and delivery address</li>
<li>Change password</li>
<li>Returns you to the product you were looking at after logging in or saving your address</li>
</ul>

<h3>Cart and checkout</h3>
<ul>
<li>Add to cart for a specific product, colour and size</li>
<li>Increase or decrease quantities in the cart</li>
<li>Checkout that creates an order and reduces stock in the database</li>
<li>Order history for each user</li>
</ul>
<h3>Reviews</h3>
<ul>
<li>Leave a star rating and written review, with an optional photo upload</li>
<li>Reply to reviews</li>
<li>Delete your reviews and replies</li>
</ul>
<h3>Wishlist and gifting</h3>
<ul>
<li>Add a specific product, colour and size to your wishlist</li>
<li>Set your wishlist to public or private</li>
<li>Search for other users' public wishlists</li>
<li>Buy an item from someone else's wishlist as a gift, which also removes it from their list</li>
</ul>
<h3>Admin panel</h3>
<ul>
<li>Add new products with images</li>
<li>Add colours and sizes to a product and set stock for each</li>
<li>Edit product details</li>
<li>Delete products, colours or sizes</li>
<li>View every order and change its status (for example, Order Placed, then Delivered or Cancelled)</li>
</ul>
